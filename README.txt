Sathishkumar V — portfolio website

HOW TO PUBLISH
Upload everything in this folder (keep the structure) to Netlify, Vercel or
Cloudflare Pages. index.html is the home page; 404.html is the not-found page.
vercel.json turns on clean URLs on Vercel (/los-toolkit instead of
/los-toolkit.html). Netlify and Cloudflare Pages do this on their own.
