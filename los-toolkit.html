Summary

From a tool to a teammate

The LOS Toolkit gave loan officers 12 reliable actions across six workflows, every one of them manual. I designed Autopilot, an AI layer that does the preparation so the loan officer only has to decide.

  • ChallengeMake a manual tool agentic, in a regulated industry
  • ApproachResearch, vibe-code, test, repeat
  • OutcomeAI layer across 6 workflows. Live.
  • Why it mattersTargets lending’s biggest cost: people’s time

09
Principles

Five rules for regulated AI

  1. 01
    AI prepares. Loan officer decides.Credit, AUS, sign-off and closing stay human.
  2. 02
    The loan officer’s word is final.AI never closes or overrides their requests.
  3. 03
    Draft before send.Nothing reaches a borrower unreviewed.
  4. 04
    Show the work.Every action has a reason and an undo.
  5. 05
    Respect the loan type.Purchase, refinance and streamline differ.

The AI workflow

Every workflow, split at the decision

Autopilot runs the preparation on its own, and stops at every regulated step. One loop, applied to six workflows.

  1. TriggerNew upload, application change, DU result
  2. Autopilot preparesReads, checks, calculates, drafts
  3. Shows the workingEvery step has a reason and a source
  4. Loan officer decidesRegulated steps stop here
  5. Logged, with undoReviewable in the activity log

Trade-offs

What I chose, and what I didn’t

  • Stop at regulated stepsFull automationBecause · Legal accountability
  • Draft before sendAuto-sendBecause · Trust beats speed
  • Lock loan officer requestsAI optimises allBecause · Pilot overrides
  • Approval queueChat on desktopBecause · Faster batch review
  • Loan-type defaultsOne task listBecause · Refinances differ
