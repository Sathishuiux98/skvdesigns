01The domain

Insurers recovering money from each other

When a driver is hit, their own insurer pays for the repair, then claims the money back from the at-fault driver’s insurer. Recover EZ is where both companies work that claim together, with evidence, approvals and a full audit trail.

✓Not-at-fault insurerOwed the money · receivable
Claim + evidenceRecovery · BHD 3,436
!At-fault insurerOwes the money · payable
  1. AccidentTwo vehicles, two insurers. One driver is at fault.
  2. Not-at-fault insurer paysIt repairs its own customer’s car first, then files a recovery.
  3. Four stagesNotification, claim inspection, liability confirmation, settlement.
  4. RecoveredA debit note goes out; a credit note closes the claim.
02The redesign

Drag to compare: the original and the redesign

Same product, same data. The redesign changes hierarchy, navigation and how a claim moves.

OriginalRedesign
What held it back
  • Icon-only top navigation and a heavy blue header
  • Charts with equal weight; no sense of what needs attention
  • A claim split into 13 icon tabs, with cards repeating the same data
What changed
  • Labelled side navigation with live counts
  • Money and “needs you” first, then trends
  • Four stages on top, sections as a checklist, a fixed summary rail

04Design decisions

Seven changes that make a claim easier to move

Decision 01Lead with what’s owed

Total receivable and payable, with “needs you” counts, sit above every chart.

WasCharts first, numbers buried
Decision 02Navigation you can read

Labelled side navigation with live counts replaces icon-only tabs.

WasFive unlabeled icons in a blue bar
Decision 03Stages, then sections

Four stages on top; that stage’s sections as a checklist on the left.

Was13 icon tabs in one row
Decision 04A summary that stays

Vehicle, amount and documents stay pinned beside the form.

WasCards repeating the same data
Decision 05Talk inside the claim

History and comments open in context, visible to both insurers.

WasA separate full-page view
Decision 06Reports you can preview

Pick columns and see the rows before you export.

WasConfigure blind, then download
Decision 07Limits that route themselves

Above a role’s limit, a claim moves to the next approval level.

WasManual hand-offs
